Curriculum Vitae

Posted at — Oct 4, 2026

Software architect with more than 20 years of experience in designing, building and running scalable web and backend systems. Since 2016 I have been responsible for architecture and implementation in long-running client projects at Leading Lights, from the business domain and microservices to Kubernetes platforms on AWS. I work pragmatically and mentor teams on architecture, code quality and testing strategy.

Core competencies

  • Architecture: design of robust, scalable backend systems, including data models, technology choices, security and quality
  • Cloud & platform: building and running Kubernetes platforms on AWS following GitOps and infrastructure-as-code principles, including observability
  • Engineering: high standards for code quality, documentation and well-thought-out testing strategies
  • Agentic coding: daily use of AI coding agents (Claude Code) with custom skills, MCP integrations and structured workflows from specification to review
  • Team: mentoring and technical leadership of development teams, close collaboration with business stakeholders
